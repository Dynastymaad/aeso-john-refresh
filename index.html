FIVE RULES — LOCKED Direction pays the bill. Percentile picks the price. Read before every ticket.
1
Trend first, always
Name the trend before you look at price. Rising, falling, or sideways — use the Percentiles Path tab. Sep-22 buys made +$4.1M; Oct-26 buys lost −$1.8M. Only difference: the trend.
2
With the trend → hit it
Any decile works. Sep-22 chase buys 80–100% averaged +$28/MWh × 19 tickets. Oct-26 chase sells 0–20% averaged +$20/MWh × 10. Take entries as they come, size up when trend is confirmed.
3
Against trend → 0–10% only
One narrow window, small size. Buying a falling market: 0–10% of range. Selling a rising market: 90–100%. Sep-22 buys at 0–10% made +$87/MWh (best on sheet). Everywhere else against the trend: zero winners on 44 tickets.
4
The 20–80% is the graveyard
No counter-trend trades in the middle. Ever. No trend support, no percentile support. Every worst ticket you've ever taken lives here. If it's not with the trend and it's not 0–10% or 90–100%: walk away.
5
Size the conviction, not the ego
Full size only when trend + percentile agree. Half-size on with-trend middle deciles. Quarter-size on the 0–10% / 90–100% counter-trend pokes. Never stack 10 tickets in 15 minutes chasing a move — that's Oct-7-2025, and it cost −$264K.
